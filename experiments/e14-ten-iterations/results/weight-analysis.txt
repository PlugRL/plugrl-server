Are the evaluated checkpoints finite, and did training move them?
Run 2026-09-22 on the cluster, against the checkpoints the evaluations loaded.

The question these answer: four cells returned 0 of 50, which would also be
the result if no checkpoint had been loaded, if the same one had been loaded
four times, or if training had driven the weights to NaN.

No NaN and no Inf anywhere, so the collapse is not numerical divergence.

The global norm is equal across checkpoints to about eight parts per million.
That is NOT evidence that the weights did not move - a norm is preserved by
any rotation - and it should not be read as such. The measurement that
answers the question is the norm of the difference, below.

furthest is the value head's first bias, by 55%: the critic starts from a
random initialisation and is still moving long after the policy has collapsed.

The base openpi checkpoint and a PlugRL checkpoint share no tensor names at
all - different serialisations, converted when the policy is constructed - so
the distance from the base weights to iteration 1 cannot be measured this way
and is not reported. That gap is why the code-path argument in FINDINGS.md is
an argument and not a measurement.
