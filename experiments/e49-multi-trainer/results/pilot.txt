2. rlinf x pendulum, first try (02:40, after E48)
   V1 True (512 episodes); learning: batches 1-2 -1231 -> 20-32 -1224 (+7): does not learn.
   RLinf's critic learned (explained variance 0.03 -> 0.95), the policy barely moved
   (approx_kl 0.002-0.004, clip fraction ~2%; SB3's was 0.03 and 0.27).
   Cause, found by reading the config against what it claims to follow: the RLinf
   Pendulum config says it uses rl-zoo's Pendulum-v1 PPO settings, but kept the
   global_batch_size 1,024 (micro 256) it inherited from RLinf's CartPole template.
   rl-zoo leaves batch_size at SB3's default, 64. With 10 epochs that is 40 updates
   per 4,096-frame rollout against rl-zoo's 640. Fixed in the config:
   global_batch_size 64, micro_batch_size 64. No other setting was changed.
   Two checks in summarise.py were wrong, and were fixed before any registered run:
   - V2 for RLinf compared env/return with batch means. RLinf logs one value per
     epoch (256 steps), the mean of the episodes that ended in it; a Pendulum epoch
     can hold two batches. Now grouped by epoch: 25 = 25, all within tolerance.
   - V3 cannot apply under RLinf. RLinf never closes its environments (E48), so the
     bridge sends no stop, and the C++ clients, killed by run_bridged.sh, never print
     their counts. PROTOCOL.md declares this.
   install_guangzhao.sh's import check ran without RLinf on PYTHONPATH and failed
   after doing its work; fixed.
