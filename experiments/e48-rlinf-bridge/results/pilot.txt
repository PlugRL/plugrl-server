What went wrong in the pilot, and was fixed before this file
  1. MuJoCoEnv runs one env per client process: --num-envs 8 failed. 16 processes (--num-procs 16).
  2. The bridge's info splitting was stricter than SPEC 5.4: an info leaf that is not per env
     (a scalar, a string) closed the connection. Now split as plugrl-server splits it (test added).
  3. Killing the env client's launcher left its 16 multiprocessing.spawn workers running. 48 of
     them, from three runs, reconnected to every later bridge; a client too many was refused with
     plugrl-server-resync, reconnected at once, and was refused again: 2,048,691 connections in
     about ten minutes (a 343 MB log) and an RLinf step slowed from 4 s to minutes.
     Fixed: the bridge refuses a client too many with plugrl-server-stop, not resync; only a
     connection holding slots counts as lost (test added, and fails without the fix); websockets'
     per-connection INFO log is off; the run script starts the clients in their own process group,
     kills the group, and refuses to start while any client process of that venv is alive.
  4. The laptop's client driver waited on ssh's stdin and outlived its run: ssh -n -o BatchMode=yes.
